Algebraic numbers are roots of polynomials with integer coefficients.

An **algebraic function** is a function that can be expressed using a finite number of operations involving the variable 'X'.
- Addition
- Subtraction
- Multiplication
- Division
- Roots (oddly enough they are based on the definition)

A **transcendental function** cannot be expressed as a finite combination of algebraic operations. 
- Logarithmic Functions
- Trignometric Functions
- Exponential Functions
- Hyperbolic Functions
